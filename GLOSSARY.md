# Cliptoboards

A place to keep deliberately saved content for later reuse.

## Language

**Clip**:
A piece of text, such as a command, URL, or reference passage, that the user has
explicitly saved for later reuse.

**Board**:
A named collection of clips. Boards are flat, and each clip belongs to exactly
one board.

**Inbox**:
The permanent board selected by default for a new capture.

**Trash**:
A recoverable holding area for deleted clips until they are restored or
permanently removed by explicitly emptying it.

**Backup**:
A portable copy of the collection used for restoration or transfer to another
Mac.

**Capture**:
The deliberate act of saving content as a clip.

**Draft**:
An unfinished capture kept separate from saved clips until explicitly saved or
discarded.
